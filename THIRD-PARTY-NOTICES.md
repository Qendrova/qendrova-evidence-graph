# Third-party dependencies

This source package pins the following npm dependencies. Installation
downloads them separately using `package-lock.json`.

| Dependency | Version | License |
|---|---|---|
| `@relate/relate` and its platform packages | 1.1.0 | Apache-2.0 |
| `koffi` and its platform packages | 3.3.8 | MIT |

Relate publishes its license and third-party notices with the [1.1.0
release](https://github.com/relatedb/relate/releases/tag/v1.1.0). The installed
Koffi 3.3.8 package contains the MIT license and copyright notice in
`LICENSE.txt`. [Koffi license information](https://koffi.dev/#license)

Keep the applicable license texts and notices with any later distribution
that includes copies of the installed dependencies or their binaries.
